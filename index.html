      About Jobsgram

      Jobsgram helps job seekers discover jobs, internships, and career opportunities from credible sources. We organize important job details into a simple, searchable format so you can evaluate opportunities with less hassle.

      Our Goal: To make job discovery easier, more organized, and more trustworthy for students, fresh graduates, and early-career professionals.

      Jobsgram Verification Standard

      • Check company and source legitimacy
      • Confirm the vacancy exists
      • Verify the application/source link
      • Review key job details
      • Check deadlines and requirements
      • Look for obvious scam indicators

      Who uses Jobsgram: Students, fresh graduates, and early-career professionals (0–5 years) looking for full-time jobs, internships, and entry-level opportunities.
